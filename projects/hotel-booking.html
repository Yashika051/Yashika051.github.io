Hotel Booking Cancellation Analytics Dashboard

PYTHON · PANDAS · KAGGLE NOTEBOOK · POWER BI

119,000+ bookings, one big question: why do so many of them fall through — and can you see it coming?

View notebook ↗

Cancellations quietly eat into a hotel's revenue forecast, but "cancellations are up" isn't actionable on its own. This project digs into over 119,000 booking records to find who cancels, when, and why — so a hotel can actually plan around it instead of getting surprised by it.

What I built

  • Cleaned and prepared the full booking dataset in Python.
  • Engineered business-friendly features — booking status, lead time groups, guest groups, and stay length groups — instead of leaving analysts to squint at raw columns.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to make the patterns explorable, not just reportable.
  • Found that City Hotels had noticeably higher cancellation rates, and that longer lead times were consistently linked to more cancellations.

Why it matters

Knowing that long-lead-time City Hotel bookings are the riskiest segment means a revenue team can adjust deposit policies or overbooking strategy for exactly that segment — instead of applying a blunt, one-size-fits-all cancellation policy across every booking.
