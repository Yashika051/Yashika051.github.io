01 · RETAIL ANALYTICS

Superstore Sales Analysis & Data Visualization

PYTHON · PANDAS · STREAMLIT

A retail dataset, a pile of questions ("why is the West region carrying the whole store?"), and a dashboard built so a manager doesn't need me in the room to get the answer.

View repository ↗

Retail data is deceptively messy — every order carries region, category, discount, and shipping info that all pull the profit number in different directions. The goal here wasn't just "make some charts," it was to make revenue and profitability legible enough that someone non-technical could poke at it and find their own answers.

What I built

  • Cleaned and structured the raw sales dataset in Python/Pandas, ready for slicing by region, category, and customer segment.
  • Built an interactive Streamlit dashboard with dynamic filters, so you can drill from "the whole store" down to "this sub-category in this region" in a couple clicks.
  • Designed KPI visualizations for revenue and profitability that surface where margin is actually being made — or quietly lost to discounts.
  • Broke performance down by region and customer segment to flag where growth is real versus where it's discount-fueled.

Why it matters

The dashboard turns "we had a good quarter" into "we had a good quarter because of these three categories in these two regions — and this one segment is only profitable because of a discount we probably shouldn't keep offering." That's the difference between a report and a decision tool.
