05 · HEALTH & SUSTAINABILITY AI

Green and Intelligent Healthcare

PYTHON · MACHINE LEARNING · AI FRAMEWORK DESIGN

Your habits affect your health. They also affect the planet. This project puts both numbers on the same screen.

View repository ↗

Health apps usually stop at "here's your risk score." This project asks a slightly bigger question: what if your lifestyle score and your environmental footprint aren't actually separate problems? Green and Intelligent Healthcare connects the two in one framework — trained on lifestyle data, not just symptom checklists.

What I built

  • Trained a machine learning model on lifestyle-based data to power the risk scoring.
  • Calculated personalized health risk scores from everyday habits.
  • Estimated carbon footprint emissions based on those same habits.
  • Connected personal wellness and environmental impact in a single AI framework, instead of treating them as unrelated apps.

Why it matters

Framing health and sustainability as one system — rather than two separate trackers — makes the tradeoffs visible. A habit that's bad for you is often bad for the planet too, and seeing both scores move together makes the case for change a lot more concrete than either number alone.
